# Tawag-Tugon — Demo Script (for LGU / Mayor's office)

**Audience:** A mayor, councilor, or DRR officer. Non-technical.
**Goal:** Show that in an emergency, a resident reaches the right responder in one tap —
even with no signal — and that the LGU controls its own content.
**Length:** ~5 minutes. Keep phone in hand; let them try it themselves.

---

## The one-line pitch
"It's your city's own emergency app — every responder's number in one tap, and it still
works even when the cell network goes down. You control what's inside, no developer needed."

## Setup before you walk in
- App pre-loaded and branded for THIS LGU (their name, seal, colors on screen).
- Real, verified local numbers already inside (barangay, police, fire, nearest hospital).
- A couple of sample announcements posted.
- **Put the phone in airplane mode before the demo** — the offline moment is the hook.

## The walkthrough
1. **Open the app.** "Notice it already looks like *your* city — your seal, your colors.
   Same app can be branded for any LGU; only the contents change."
2. **The directory.** "Here's every first responder, grouped and ordered by what matters
   most — fire, police, barangay at the top."
3. **The one tap (the moment).** Point out airplane mode is ON. Tap a responder → the
   phone's dialer opens instantly. "No signal, no internet — and it still works. That's the
   whole point. In a real disaster, people don't have time to search their contacts."
4. **Viber / Messenger.** Show a contact that opens Viber or Messenger. "Whatever channel a
   responder actually uses, it's one tap away."
5. **Local news.** "Your office can post announcements — advisories, relief schedules,
   evacuation info — and residents see them right here, even offline after the last update."
6. **You're in control.** Show the simple admin. "Your staff update numbers and post
   announcements themselves. No calling a developer every time something changes."

## Close
"This is a working version for one city. To bring it to yours, we just need your official
numbers, your seal and colors, and your first announcements — and we can brand it for your
LGU. It's built to scale across barangays and districts as budget allows."

## If they ask… (quick answers)
- **"Does it cost residents data?"** No — it works offline; it only syncs updates when online.
- **"What if numbers change?"** Your staff edit them anytime; residents get the update on next sync.
- **"iPhone and Android?"** Yes, one app for both.
- **"What about a total blackout / no towers?"** The directory still works offline today.
  Longer-range device-to-device features are on the roadmap, not part of this version.
- **"How fast can we launch?"** Once we have your numbers, seal/colors, and first
  announcements, branding your version is quick.

## Do / Don't
- **Do** let them tap it themselves. **Do** keep it about safety and control, not tech.
- **Don't** promise mesh networking, push alerts, or hazard-agency integrations — those are
  future, not V1. Under-promise, over-deliver.
